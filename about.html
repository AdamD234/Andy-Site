About City Stone Masonry

Preserving History, Perfecting Homes

We offer stone cleaning and restoration as well as repointing both brickwork and stone. We consider ourselves to be experts at making your property striking, if you're ready for your home to reach its full potential, just give us a call!

At City Stone Masonry, our focus is always on combining timeless artisan craft with cutting-edge preservation tools. From traditional lime mortars that breathe with historic structures to precise architectural detailing, our reputation is built stone by stone.
