Hi! My name is Andrew, I am the director of City Stone Masonry. I have over 25 years of experience in the stone masonry industry. I started City Stone Masonry with the aim of providing customers with high quality, reasonable prices and an overall great experience.

I built the company myself, working tirelessly to get where we are now. At the heart of the company is our values; I want every customer to feel delighted with the end results of their property improvements. I am very proud of the progress I have made and I am excited to see what the future has in store for City Stone Masonry!
